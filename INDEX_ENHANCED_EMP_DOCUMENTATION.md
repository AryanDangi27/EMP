## 🎯 Content Overview

### Form Structure

**6 Sections:**
1. 🔵 Patient Identity (5 fields, all required)
2. 🔴 Emergency Contacts (2 fields, 1 required)
3. 🟠 Allergies (checkboxes + custom)
4. 🟢 Chronic Conditions (checkboxes + custom)
5. 🟣 Medical History (2 textareas, optional)
6. 🔵 Digital Linking (optional)

**Key Features:**
- ✅ Checkbox selections for common items
- ✅ Custom entry for unlisted items
- ✅ Multiple emergency contacts
- ✅ Complete medical history
- ✅ Professional appearance
- ✅ Easy for hospital staff

## 📝 Required Fields (6)

1. **Full Name** - Patient's legal name
2. **Email** - Contact email address
3. **Date of Birth** - Patient's birth date
4. **Gender** - Select from dropdown
